Comprehensive Audit of Current Website

Our engineering team has performed an exhaustive A-to-Z structural, technical, and content extraction audit of your existing website (welcomeconsultancy.in) to identify critical growth blockers and outline essential preservation requirements.

Content & Architecture Inventory (To Be Exactly Preserved)

To guarantee **zero disruption to existing search rankings (SEO)** and ensure seamless continuity for returning clients, our development team will manually migrate and enhance the complete existing service structure across all **16 dedicated service paths**:

Core Licensing & Registrations:
  • • Import - Export Code (IEC Code)
  • • Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG License)
  • • Advance Authorization / Licence Scheme
  • • Export House Certificate / Star Export House
  • • RCMC Application (Export Promotion Councils)
  • • AD Code & ICEGATE Registration Services
Specialized Compliance & Incentives:
  • • Export Incentives (RoDTEP / RoSCTL / Drawback)
  • • Authorized Economic Operator (AEO Registration)
  • • Certificate of Origin (C.O.O.) & Digital Signature
  • • FSSAI Registration (For Export & Import)
  • • SIMS & REX Registration Services
  • • Health Certificate & Interest Equalization Scheme

Technical & Structural Issues Identified (Why Redesign is Vital)

Broken Quick Links & Placeholders
  • 8 Placeholder Sidebar Links: The "Quick Links" section on the homepage currently lists 8 identical links labelled "Import of Pets" that all point to broken anchors (#), undermining corporate credibility.
  • Empty Email Hyperlink: The header contact bar features an empty href="mailto:"` tag with no email address assigned, preventing clients from clicking to email inquiries.
Heavy Performance Bloat
  • 30+ HTTP CSS Requests: The legacy WordPress Elementor setup loads more than 30 individual stylesheets and 12 font files sequentially, causing noticeable render-blocking lag.
  • Outdated Asset Dependencies: Reliance on older jQuery scripts, redundant Flaticon libraries (8 versions loaded simultaneously), and unoptimized stock background images.
SEO & Visibility Deficits
  • Missing Structured Schema: No JSON-LD LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService schema markup exists to help Google display rich business details or star ratings in search results.
  • Generic Blog Thumbnails: News articles rely on 2019 theme demo placeholder thumbnails (theme-10.jpg, theme-07.jpg) rather than authentic trade graphics.
