Preface

Introduction

We researched multiple websites across different industries. Instead of limiting inspiration to import-export companies, we also explored global consulting firms, enterprise SaaS products, premium agencies, and award-winning websites. This allows us to build a modern website that not only meets industry expectations but also stands apart from competitors.

The purpose of this document is to provide design references and inspiration so that you can choose your preferred design direction before we begin the UI/UX design phase. We invite you to explore the following sections and share which styles, color treatments, and layouts resonate best with your vision.

Section 07

Recommended Design Direction

Based on our research across multiple industries, we have outlined the recommended visual system, palette, typography, and motion guidelines for the Welcome Consultancy platform.

Design Style

  • Modern Corporate & Minimalist
  • Generous whitespace to feel premium
  • Clean cards with subtle drop shadows
  • Professional grids and enterprise borders
  • High-quality lifestyle photography

Color Palette

Professional, authority-rich color palette utilizing dark navy backgrounds, light gray panels, and crisp blue accents.

Navy
#0F172A
Accent Blue
#2563EB
Light Gray
#F8FAFC
White
#FFFFFF

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans
Used for premium headings, titles, and cards. (Extremely clean & corporate)
Inter Sans
Used for paragraphs, description text, lists, and forms. (High readability)

Animation Guidelines

  • Subtle card lift on hover
  • Smooth opacity fades on load
  • Color transition on buttons
  • Clean tab/filter fade shifts
  • No heavy parallax or distracting loading spinners to maintain corporate dignity
Section 08

Final Recommendation

A Cohesive, Cross-Industry Strategy

Instead of replicating a single competitor's platform, we strongly recommend a selective integration strategy. By taking the strongest layout and interface paradigms from different industries, we can create a unique, premium trade consultancy portal that establishes industry authority in India.

Hero Section

Inspired by the McKinsey editorial layout. Clean sans-serif hierarchy, lots of whitespace, and professional lifestyle photography.

Service Showcase

Inspired by the Airtable and Stripe grid structures. Services are displayed using modern grid blocks with crisp border outlines and color pills.

Navigation & Footer

Inspired by Linear. High contrast, sticky minimal headers, clean logo margins, and a detailed multi-column footer.

Trust Indicators

Inspired by Deloitte and PwC. Prominently displays the partner logos, IndiaMart / JustDial certifications, and client testimonials with high-end typography.

This multi-reference approach ensures the new Welcome Consultancy website remains extremely clean, modern, and trustworthy, directly supporting the company's 10+ years of trade consulting expertise.
